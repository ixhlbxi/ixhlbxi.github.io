chefbooger-backup — Privacy Policy

chefbooger-backup is used only by its owner. It accesses the owner's own Google Drive solely to upload, list and delete backup files that it stores there.

It does not read, collect, store, sell or share any other data, and it does not access any other person's account.

Google user data obtained through Google APIs is used only for this purpose and is not transferred to anyone.
